# Loan Origination Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines the major information and control stages used during loan origination in the fictional CloudFin policy set.

## Scope
It covers application intake, identity checks, documentation, assessment handoffs, approval controls, and pre-disbursement validation.

## Definitions
**Origination** is the process from receiving a credit application through approved account setup or disbursement. **Application package** is the set of information and evidence required for assessment.

## Procedures
1. Capture applicant identity and contact information.
2. Record the requested product, amount, purpose, and relevant term information.
3. Complete KYC and required customer due diligence.
4. Obtain income, employment, business, or cash-flow evidence appropriate to the product.
5. Record existing financial obligations and supporting statements where required.
6. Route the complete case to credit assessment under the Credit Risk Policy.
7. Confirm approval conditions and required documents before disbursement.

## Requirements
Applications must be complete enough to support an independent review. Missing mandatory documents must be resolved before final processing. Approval conditions must be recorded and satisfied before funds are released.

## Responsibilities
Front-line teams collect application information. Operations validate completeness. Credit teams perform risk assessment. Authorized approvers make decisions within delegated limits. Disbursement teams confirm conditions are satisfied.

## Escalation and Exceptions
Suspected fraud, unexplained document alterations, material application inconsistencies, or policy exceptions must be escalated. CloudFin AI may explain these policy steps but must not approve, decline, or recommend a specific loan for a person.
