# AML Policy

## Purpose
This policy establishes Anti-Money Laundering (AML) controls for identifying, monitoring, and escalating activity that may require further review.

## Scope
It applies to customer onboarding, transaction monitoring, customer reviews, staff escalation, and case handling across covered products.

## Definitions
**Unusual activity** is behavior that differs materially from the expected profile and lacks a clear explanation. **Suspicious activity** is unusual activity that, after review, presents indicators requiring formal escalation under applicable procedures.

## Procedures
Customer risk factors are assessed during onboarding and refreshed when material information changes. Monitoring alerts are reviewed using customer profile, transaction context, counterparties, geography, and known source-of-funds information. Staff must document facts rather than assumptions.

## Common Escalation Indicators
Examples include unexplained rapid movement of funds, repeated transactions designed to avoid internal thresholds, activity inconsistent with the stated business purpose, unusual third-party funding, unexplained high-risk geographic exposure, or refusal to provide required due-diligence information. A single indicator does not automatically prove wrongdoing.

## Requirements
Potential concerns must be handled confidentially. Staff must not alert customers that an AML investigation or regulatory report may be considered. Records supporting a review must be retained in accordance with the organization's retention rules.

## Responsibilities
Operations review routine alerts. Compliance performs enhanced investigation and determines whether additional internal or regulatory action is required. Employees must promptly report concerns through approved channels.

## Escalation and Exceptions
Material red flags, sanctions matches, suspected identity fraud, or patterns that cannot be reasonably explained must be escalated to Compliance. Business convenience is not a valid reason to suppress an AML escalation.
