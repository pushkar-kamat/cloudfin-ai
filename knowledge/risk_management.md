# Risk Management Policy

## Purpose
This policy provides a simple framework for identifying, assessing, treating, and monitoring operational and compliance risks relevant to the CloudFin demonstration organization.

## Scope
It applies to significant processes, technology changes, third-party dependencies, policy exceptions, and material operational incidents.

## Definitions
**Inherent risk** is exposure before controls. **Residual risk** is exposure after considering controls. **Risk owner** is the person accountable for monitoring and treating an identified risk.

## Procedures
Teams identify material risks, describe causes and potential impact, assess likelihood and impact using the approved internal scale, document existing controls, and define treatment actions where residual risk is above tolerance. Open actions should have owners and target dates.

## Requirements
Risk assessments should be evidence-based and refreshed after material change. Significant dependencies on cloud services, authentication providers, and external AI services should be documented. Known control failures should not be treated as effective controls.

## Responsibilities
Business owners manage risks in their processes. Risk functions provide frameworks and independent challenge. Technology owners manage service and security risks. Senior management reviews significant residual risks.

## Escalation and Exceptions
Risks exceeding approved tolerance, repeated incidents, major service outages, or overdue critical actions must be escalated through the appropriate governance route.
