# Credit Risk Policy

## Purpose
This policy describes a controlled approach for assessing borrower credit risk in the CloudFin demonstration environment. It is informational and does not automate lending decisions.

## Scope
It applies to internal borrower assessment, credit review, documentation quality, and monitoring concepts used in the policy knowledge base.

## Definitions
**Credit risk** is the risk of financial loss if a borrower or counterparty does not meet agreed obligations. **Capacity** refers to the ability to repay. **Collateral** refers to assets supporting an exposure where applicable.

## Procedures
A borrower assessment considers verified identity, stated purpose, income or cash-flow evidence, existing obligations, repayment history where lawfully available, and relevant business or financial information. Analysts should consider both quantitative information and documented qualitative factors.

## Requirements
Credit assessment must be based on current, supportable information. Material assumptions should be documented. Missing information, contradictory evidence, or significant deterioration must be identified before a case proceeds. Credit risk review should remain separate from sales incentives where practical.

## Responsibilities
Business teams collect complete application information. Credit staff assess risk under approved criteria. Independent risk or delegated authorities review higher-risk or exception cases.

## Escalation and Exceptions
Significant data inconsistencies, suspected fraud, material policy exceptions, or exposures outside delegated authority must be escalated to the appropriate credit or risk authority. This policy does not authorize personalized lending decisions through CloudFin AI.
