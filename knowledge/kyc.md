# KYC Policy

## Purpose
This policy defines the minimum Know Your Customer (KYC) controls used by the fictional CloudFin demonstration organization to establish and verify a customer's identity before a regulated relationship is activated.

## Scope
The policy applies to onboarding, material account changes, periodic reviews, and re-verification events for individual and business customers handled by covered teams.

## Definitions
**Customer identification** means collecting core identity details. **Verification** means confirming those details using acceptable independent evidence. **Beneficial owner** means an individual who ultimately owns or controls a legal entity.

## Procedures
1. Collect the customer's full legal name, date of birth or incorporation date, contact details, and residential or registered address.
2. Obtain an approved identity document and an approved address-verification document when required by the customer type.
3. For business customers, identify the legal entity, authorized signatories, ownership structure, and beneficial owners.
4. Compare submitted information for consistency and record the verification outcome.
5. Complete sanctions and risk screening under the applicable compliance procedure before activation.

## Requirements
Identity evidence must be valid, readable, and appropriate to the customer type. Address evidence should be current according to internal operating guidance. Accounts must not be activated while mandatory identity checks remain incomplete. Higher-risk relationships may require enhanced due diligence.

## Responsibilities
Front-line teams collect required information. Operations staff verify documentation and record outcomes. Compliance defines approved evidence standards and reviews exceptions or higher-risk cases.

## Escalation and Exceptions
Unexplained document inconsistencies, suspected impersonation, sanctions concerns, or repeated verification failures must be escalated to Compliance. Exceptions require documented approval and must not bypass mandatory legal or sanctions controls.
