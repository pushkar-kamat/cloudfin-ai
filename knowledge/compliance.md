# Compliance Policy

## Purpose
This policy describes the governance principles used to support adherence to internal policies and applicable regulatory obligations within the CloudFin demonstration organization.

## Scope
It applies to employees, contractors, covered business processes, policy management, issue escalation, and compliance monitoring.

## Definitions
**Compliance obligation** means a requirement arising from applicable law, regulation, license condition, or approved internal policy. **Control** means a process designed to prevent, detect, or respond to compliance risk.

## Procedures
Business owners identify obligations relevant to their processes and implement documented controls. Compliance provides interpretation, monitoring, advice, and independent challenge. Material issues are tracked to closure with an owner and target date.

## Requirements
Policies should have clear ownership, periodic review, and version control. Staff must complete required training and promptly report suspected breaches. Compliance records must be accurate and available for authorized review.

## Responsibilities
Business teams own day-to-day compliance with controls. Compliance advises and monitors. Senior management oversees material risk and remediation. Internal assurance functions may test control effectiveness independently.

## Escalation and Exceptions
Actual or suspected serious breaches, repeated control failures, regulator communications, or unresolved high-risk exceptions must be escalated through the approved governance route. Exceptions must be time-limited and documented.
